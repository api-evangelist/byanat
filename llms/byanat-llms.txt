# Byanat

> Byanat is an Oman-based, award-winning AI company offering a unified platform for optimizing digital infrastructure across telecommunications networks and data centers. The platform combines system-wide visibility with tailored automation to scale operations, ensure compliance, and promote sustainability ("Infrastructure 4.0").

This llms.txt was generated by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline from the public Byanat website. Byanat does not publish a public developer portal, OpenAPI specification, or API reference at this time; its "API & Integration" offering is a managed product rather than a self-serve public API.

## Products
- [Mobile Network Solutions](https://byanat.ai/products/mobile-networks): Site management, degradation detection, auto-discovery, and performance analytics for mobile operators.
- [Data Centre Solutions](https://byanat.ai/products/data-centres): Predictive analytics, capacity forecasting, energy management, and infrastructure monitoring.
- [API & Integration](https://byanat.ai/products/apis-integration): Unified API lifecycle management, cross-platform integration, and AI-powered workflow automation with encryption, audit trails, and GDPR/HIPAA support.
