We are honest with you, as a talented engineer looking for a job in the technology industry, you might find yourself in a position to choose a company you want to be part of rather than the other way around. This also probably means you want to avoid wasting time with irrelevant hiring processes. We share these thoughts too, and we’ve created our process so that it provides us with a thorough skill assessment and gives you a transparent understanding of what your day to day life at Byanat could look like.
